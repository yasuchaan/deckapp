ScheduleDeck Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026
Provider: Shun Yasuda

1. Scope and approach

This policy explains how ScheduleDeck (the “app”) and its support contact handle information. The provider is Shun Yasuda. Information processed on your device is distinguished from information received by the provider, for example through support inquiries.

2. Information on your device

Schedule titles, dates, times, types and locations, LISTs and associated information, TODO text, due dates and completion status, imported documents, handwriting, text and settings are stored and processed on your device to provide, edit, display and recover app data. Their contents are not uploaded to the announcement endpoint. If you choose to export, share or attach information to a support email, it is passed to the recipient or destination you select.

3. Calendars, TODOs and widgets

The app requests OS permission to access device calendars. It reads permitted event titles, dates, times, locations, calendar names and identifiers for display and linking. At your request it creates or edits events in writable external calendars. Information about linked external events may also be saved in the app. iCloud, Google and other calendars use accounts configured on the device through OS EventKit; the app does not directly synchronize by asking you to enter your Google password. External calendar storage and synchronization follow the settings and terms of Apple, Google or other providers. This is not continuous iCloud synchronization of all app schedules, LISTs, documents and handwriting. The app’s own TODOs are managed locally and are not automatically synchronized between devices. For widgets, event titles, dates, times and locations, and TODO titles, due dates, completion status and related information are copied to a shared area on the same device. Depending on device settings, content can appear on the lock screen; check widget placement and display settings. This is not cross-device synchronization. Selecting “Open in Maps” passes the location text to Apple Maps.

4. Integration between the apps

The app reads ScoreDeck SETLIST names and identifiers and saves associated event dates, times, types and identifiers and LIST names in a shared area on the device. ScoreDeck reads this information to display associated SETLISTs or mini SETLISTs. TODOs are not included in this integration catalog. Links that open the other app pass target identifiers, dates or similar information. This is sharing on the same device, not uploading to a developer server or synchronizing between devices.

5. Backups and external storage

Backup and restore features copy and restore app data. If you select iCloud Drive or another destination in Files, the backup is passed to that destination and its service terms apply. Backups are distinct from continuous synchronization. OS device backup handling depends on device and account settings.

6. Announcements and external pages

The app makes HTTPS requests to yasuchaan.github.io/deckapp/announcements.json on GitHub Pages to retrieve announcements. The destination receives connection information such as your IP address. Announcement cache, read status and retrieval times are stored locally. Score, schedule, TODO and handwriting contents are not added to these requests. When you open an external page from an announcement or other link, consult that provider’s policy as well.

7. Support and bug reports

When you email deck.app88@gmail.com, the provider receives your sender address, display name, subject, message and any information you attach. These are used to respond, communicate with you and investigate and improve faults. Do not send unnecessary personal information or documents you have no right to share. The receiving mailbox uses Google Gmail; your own email service and other services involved in transmission also handle the message.

8. Third-party disclosure and security

Information received through support is not disclosed to third parties except with your consent, where required by law or in other legally permitted circumstances. When external providers process information to deliver services, we apply the necessary oversight in accordance with applicable law. We take necessary and appropriate security measures under applicable law to prevent leakage, loss or damage.

9. Retention, deletion and permissions

Manage local data using app deletion and per-item deletion features. Prior backups, pre-restoration data, exported files, OS backups, integration data and external service data may not all be deleted together. Manage unnecessary copies at each destination. Calendar access can be changed in OS settings. Revoking permission does not necessarily erase linked information already saved or backups. Support information is used for support, fault investigation and improvement, and necessary communication. We do not set a fixed retention period: we retain it while needed for these purposes and appropriately delete it when no longer needed. If retention is legally required, we retain it for the period needed for that purpose. To request access, correction, deletion or restriction of use, contact support below. We may verify your identity and respond under applicable law. For local or external-service data that the provider cannot access, we will guide you to the relevant features or provider.

10. Changes and contact

If information handling changes, we will announce the changes and effective date on this page or in the app and obtain consent where required by law. ScheduleDeck support: deck.app88@gmail.com. Provider: Shun Yasuda.

11. About this website

This site has no advertisements, analytics tags or input forms. When you view a page, GitHub Pages receives connection information such as your IP address. The hosting provider’s handling of information is governed by the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Bug report email drafts

When you choose Report a Bug in the app, the app name, version, build number and iPadOS version are prefilled in an email draft. You can review and edit the draft before sending. This information is not sent to the provider until you choose to send the email.
